# Spawnpoint

**Live:** https://anishohmyghosh.github.io/spawnpoint/

The exact moment and place you entered the game. Spawnpoint lays astrology, Human Design, numerology, the Chinese zodiac and the Enneagram over one star wheel, then shows where the systems agree.

- **Calculated, not looked up.** Sun, Moon and Rising, the Human Design bodygraph (type, authority, profile, channels, all 64 gates), Life Path, Expression and Soul Urge, and your Chinese sign are all computed in the browser from birth date, time and place, using [astronomy-engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy). Nothing is sent anywhere; your chart is saved only in your own browser.
- **The full sky.** Every planet in its sign, the 12 Placidus houses, Ascendant and Midheaven on a clickable chart wheel with a plain-language reading of each planet and house (checked against Swiss Ephemeris to within 0.01°). Without a birth time, planets are shown in their signs only.
- **Spawn as someone else.** A carousel of famous charts (Carl Jung, Frida Kahlo, Priyanka Chopra, Bruce Lee, Yoko Ono, Beyoncé, Maya Angelou, David Bowie, Wangari Maathai, Hayao Miyazaki, Rabindranath Tagore and more). Birth times come from birth records or highly rated sources; where no reliable time exists, the chart is drawn for noon and says so.
- **Explore the bodygraph.** Tap any center, channel or gate for a plain-language explanation.
- **Play the reading.** A guided tour that lights up one section at a time with a read-along caption. In Chrome it is narrated by the Google UK English Female voice.
- **Save as PDF.** The whole map, every reading, the detailed bodygraph and all ten life areas.

These systems are lenses for reflection, not facts. Keep what resonates and drop what doesn't.

Single file, no build step: open `index.html`.
